# ReadyNest Demo Script — target 2:15

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook

**Narration:**
"Most assistants answer emergency-preparedness questions. ReadyNest does the harder thing: it keeps the household moving until the plan is finished."

Show the ReadyNest screen and the persistent-memory badge.

## 0:15–0:48 — One request → many actions

Click **Build 48-hour plan**.

**Narration:**
"I give it one request. ReadyNest loads the household context, audits our fictional supplies, checks route state, assigns tasks, schedules a check-in, and creates a prioritized action board."

Pause on the activity log and task board.

**Narration:**
"Notice the confirmation gate. It can prepare a supply action, but it does not cross a consequential boundary without the user."

## 0:48–1:20 — Autonomous replanning

Click **Situation changed**.

**Narration:**
"Now the situation changes. Landfall moves up eighteen hours and the primary route becomes congested. ReadyNest does not start over. It updates the same plan, switches the fictional route, moves deadlines forward, and promotes the pet kit and documents to critical."

Point to ETA, route, task priorities, and planner actions.

## 1:20–1:45 — State across sessions

Click **New session**, then **Resume from memory**.

**Narration:**
"This is a new session. The conversation is gone, but the household plan is not. ReadyNest restores the state and gives a compact status instead of making the user explain everything again."

## 1:45–2:05 — Human control

Click **Confirm in simulation**.

**Narration:**
"When I explicitly confirm, it completes only the simulated supply step. No payment, message, route guidance, or external API is used in this hackathon build."

## 2:05–2:15 — Close

**Narration:**
"ReadyNest shows what an Alexa+ experience can become when conversation is not the product—the completed household outcome is."
